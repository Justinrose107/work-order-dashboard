Counting rules and region mapping

Work orders with a Closed, Close, Cancelled or Canceled status (including Chinese equivalents) are excluded. A Completed flow status does not mean the work order is closed. Duplicate work order numbers are retained and flagged. All counts represent source rows.

Timestamps without a timezone use the selected timezone (UTC+8 by default). Counts refresh every minute. Missing completion times are shown separately because their elapsed time cannot be determined. Imported spreadsheet data and filter preferences are saved locally in this browser; use Clear saved data to remove them.
